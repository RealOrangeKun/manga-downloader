# Code of Conduct ♡

Nyanga is a small, cozy project. Let's keep it that way, in issues, pull requests and anywhere
else we talk about it.

## Be kind

- Be friendly and patient, especially with people who are new to coding or to GitHub.
- Assume good intent. If something reads as rude, ask before you react.
- Critique code and ideas, not people. Say what would make it better.
- Accept that the maintainer has the final say on what goes into Nyanga.

## Not okay

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
- Hateful or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background or body.
- Sexual content or unwanted attention.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, trolling or repeatedly pushing a point after being asked to stop.
- Asking for or posting links to pirated content in issues.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to the maintainer, [@RealOrangeKun](https://github.com/RealOrangeKun), by opening an
issue or contacting them through their GitHub profile. Reports are kept private.

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, or block someone
from the project, depending on how serious it is. Usually a friendly heads-up comes first.

This applies to everyone taking part in the project's spaces, including the maintainer ✿
